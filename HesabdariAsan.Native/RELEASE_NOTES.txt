حسابداری آسان Native v4 — Phase 4
Version: 4.0.0-alpha.4

- Purchase/stock-entry module added with cash/credit supplier accounting.
- Purchase history added with true database pagination (10/20/50).
- Safe invoice editing added: quantity, sale price, line removal, discount.
- Invoice cancellation added without deleting financial history.
- Inventory and customer debt reversal are transactional during invoice edit/cancel.
- Credit invoices are protected from unsafe edits/cancellation after later debt receipts.
- Cancelled invoices are excluded from dashboard/report financial totals and profit charts.
- Cancelled receipts print with a clear cancellation marker.
- Native audit log page added with database pagination.
- Audit logging expanded to invoices, products, parties, debt payments, expenses, purchases, settings, restore and migration.
- Invoice revision snapshots added for create/edit/cancel lifecycle.
- Parties with debt or financial history can no longer be destructively deleted.
- Duplicate active product barcodes are blocked.
- Schema upgraded to v4 with safe migration from Phase 3 columns.
- Stress-tested SQLite model with 5,000 products, 12,000 invoices, 36,000 invoice items, 3,500 expenses and 2,501 purchases; quick_check OK.
- Version bumped to 4.0.0-alpha.4.
